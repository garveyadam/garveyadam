2. Fee Structure and Payment Terms

The firm works under one of two payment arrangements. Your invoice, payment page, or engagement letter shows which one applies to your matter. Unless we have agreed in writing to bill you after the work is done, payment in advance applies.

A. Payment in Advance

This applies to new clients and to limited-scope matters, such as an individual trademark application, Office Action response, or maintenance filing.

  • Flat Fees: Most matters are handled for a flat fee stated in the invoice, payment page, or engagement letter. A flat fee is a fixed price for the services described. It does not change with the time the work takes.
  • Payment Due: Fees and government fees are due on receipt of the invoice or payment page. We do not begin work or make a filing until payment is received.
  • Instruction to Proceed: Paying the invoice or completing the payment page is your instruction to the firm to proceed with the services described there, and your agreement to these Terms and Conditions. If we determine after payment that we cannot perform those services, for example because of a conflict of interest or because the filing cannot properly be made, we will tell you promptly and refund you as described below.
  • Flat Fee Deposits and Refunds: Flat fees are deposited in the firm's operating account. You may instead require that a flat fee be held in a client trust account until it is earned. If the representation ends or the services are not completed, you are entitled to a refund of any part of the fee that has not been earned.
  • Not a Retainer: Payments are for the specific services described. They are not a retainer for the firm's general availability.
  • Government Fees and Costs: Government filing fees and other third-party costs are charged separately, at cost, and must be paid before the filing is made. If a cost is not incurred, or is less than the amount collected, the difference is refunded to you or credited to your next invoice, at your choice.

B. Billing After the Work

This applies where we have agreed in writing to bill after the work is performed, usually for clients with an established relationship with the firm and for ongoing matters such as litigation.

  • Fees: Fees are charged at the hourly rates or flat fees stated in your engagement letter or otherwise agreed in writing.
  • Invoices: We invoice monthly or when a matter is completed. Payment is due on receipt of the invoice.
  • Costs: We may pay government filing fees and other third-party costs on your behalf and include them on your invoice at cost.
  • Advance Deposits: If we ask for an advance deposit, it is held in the firm's client trust account and applied to your invoices as described in your engagement letter.
  • Change to Payment in Advance: If invoices are not paid when due, we may require payment in advance for further work.
  • Engagement Letter Controls: If your engagement letter differs from these Terms and Conditions, the engagement letter controls.

Payment Processing Fee
