01 — Core Services

Intellectual Property Services

Comprehensive IP counsel across patents, trademarks, copyrights, and trade secrets— designed to protect innovation, build defensible portfolios, and support business growth.

PATENT LAW

Patent Filing & Prosecution

Strategic utility and design patent applications. We help inventors secure robust protection for mechanical, electrical, software, and medical device innovations. Our prosecution strategy is designed to maximize claim scope and withstand validity challenges.

TRADEMARK LAW

Registration & Enforcement

Comprehensive clearance searches, USPTO applications, and Office Action responses. We handle TTAB opposition proceedings to defend your brand identity and advise on international registration through the Madrid Protocol.

COPYRIGHT

Copyright & Software IP

Registration and licensing for creative works, SaaS platforms, and source code. Secure ownership rights and handle DMCA takedown notices.

TRADE SECRETS

Trade Secret Protection

Drafting NDAs and confidentiality agreements. We help companies audit trade secrets and pursue misappropriation claims.

PORTFOLIO

Global IP Strategy

Long-term IP planning and foreign filing (PCT/Madrid Protocol). Build defensible assets that support valuation and exits.

DUE DILIGENCE

M&A & Licensing

IP audits for mergers and acquisitions. We structure technology transfer agreements and assess IP risks for investors.
